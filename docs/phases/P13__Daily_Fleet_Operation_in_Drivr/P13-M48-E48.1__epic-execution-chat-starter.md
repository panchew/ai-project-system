## Spec References (load-one-reference-the-other, AOG §3.1.1)

**Full spec:** `docs/phases/P13__Daily_Fleet_Operation_in_Drivr/P13-M48-E48.1__spec__operational-evidence-and-run-gate.md`

The Epic spec is the authoritative copy of **Deliverables (D1–D6), Definition of Done, Acceptance
Criteria, Binding Constraints, Non-Goals, and Notes.** **This starter does not duplicate them.**
Cite the spec by path and branch — **not** by a version number or sha.

**The six things easiest to get wrong, flagged here and specified there:**

1. **Model identity vs executable route.** `remote:deepseek-v4-flash` names a model, not the
   `opencode/` vs `opencode-go/` catalog route. Record both, in distinct fields.
2. **Reading credential values.** Presence and effective path category only. Emit nothing.
3. **Double-counting provider totals.** A step total and its components overlap; never sum both;
   raw counts and economic evidence reported separately.
4. **Designing the bar after the result.** The bar is committed before dispatch, never after.
5. **Inventing missing telemetry.** Absent data is `unavailable` with a reason, never zero-filled.
6. **Using a raw `opencode run` as proof.** The proven path is `OpenCodeAdapter.execute`; a raw CLI
   invocation alone does not prove reuse of that path.
