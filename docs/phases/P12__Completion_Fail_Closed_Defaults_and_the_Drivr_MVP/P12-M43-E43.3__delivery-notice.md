surface falls out of its reach.** E43.3 writes the one statement of the rework limit —
the 3-attempt maximum *and* what a written extension grants (exactly one further

starter-shaped surfaces, each with its state (carry for the two Milestone surfaces,

falsified twice on the branch, fails when a listed surface neither carries nor cites the
statement, and when the "resets" semantics reappear. The `P12-GH-1` carry-forward note

## Deliverables

### D1 — The one normative statement, placed ✅

PROJECT-SYSTEM-GUIDELINES.md **§11.6 "The Rework Limit"** — a parent may reject a
child's delivery a **maximum of 3 attempts**; on a third still-unacceptable Completion
Notice, no fourth rejection-and-retry — the child produces an **Escalation Notice** and
the parent escalates; **silent fourth attempts are a governance violation**. **A written
extension grants exactly ONE further attempt, not a reset to three.** Also defines
**rework exhaustion** (3 attempts + any written `+1`, without an acceptable delivery) —
the state E43.4's flip triggers on. **Placement decision recorded:** the normative tier
(PSG §11.6) — the rule was in **no** normative document (W2), which argued for the
normative tier, not for leaving it in a starter; it sits with the acceptance chain's
other two structural facts (E43.1 parent-merges, E43.2 attributable acknowledgment).
Version 2.5.0 → 2.6.0.

`systems/milestone-execution-chat-starter.md`'s Rework Cycle: *"The 3-attempt limit
resets only if you explicitly grant an extension in writing"* is rewritten to
**"a written extension grants exactly ONE further attempt, not a reset to three"**
(SN-36/37, CFO-decided, stricter). One statement survives; the drift is resolved, not
annotated (Binding Constraint 5). Version 1.3.0 → 1.4.0.
