# Delivery Notice: P12-M43-E43.2 — Acceptance Distinguishable from Absence

> **`merge_details` is partially unfillable at delivery time.** A Delivery Notice is

## Summary

**Silence is no longer the sole carrier of acceptance.** A clean delivery is now accepted
by an **in-chat acknowledgment that names the party that reviewed and accepted** (role +
session identity) — a positive, attributable signal recorded with the parent's merge
(E43.1). **Silence accepts nothing.** Default-accept is tweaked, not retired: a clean
delivery still produces **no new artifact** — the signal rides the §11.6 acknowledgment
that already exists, so *reviewed and clean* is distinguishable from *nobody looked* from
the record alone, and a duplicated role leaves two committed, attributable lines rather
than two indistinguishable silences. PSG §11.6 is amended (with a stated boundary: the
signal does **not** make the review good); AOG §12 and every live surface stating the
model agree; `chat-hierarchy.md:201-205` is reconciled (the attendance presumption is
replaced by the attributable acknowledgment, the manual/agentic line survives); and a new
check — falsified three ways, not assumed — asserts no surface may accept a clean delivery
by silence.

## Deliverables

### D1 — The decision, with options weighed (including rejected options) ✅

The mechanism and its surface were delegated to this epic; they were decided, documented,
and proceeded (the record §1). **Chosen:** a clean delivery is accepted by an in-chat
acknowledgment that **names the party that reviewed and accepted** (role + session
identity), recorded with the parent's merge; silence accepts nothing. **Rejected options,
each with the reason:** an explicit acceptance artifact per delivery (adds a happy-path
artifact — fails the Hard Constraint); an absence attributed to a role (reproduces the
fourth-state defect at one remove); a separate acceptance-log artifact (a new object on
the happy path); a runtime-only signal (violates the committed-record invariant). HQ's
2026-08-21 M41-only stopgap is the recorded evidence that cheapness and auditability are
compatible; the design generalizes it from a suspension log to the acknowledgment itself.

### D2 — The normative change in PSG §11.6 and wherever default-accept is stated ✅

PSG §11.6 (The Model happy path; What Default-Accept Governs; gate (B); two new
subsections — "What the Signal Does Not Claim" and "Where the Acknowledgment Is
Recorded"), §1A gate-scoping, §5C Step 6, §11.5 flow + Key Rule 4, §12, §13A, §13B —
version 2.4.1 → **2.5.0**. AOG §12 (outcome-1 + normative block), §3.6, §3.7, §10 —
version 2.10.2 → **2.11.0**. Every live surface stating the model was reconciled —
**itemized** (seventeen surfaces, each with post-change state) in the record §2.1; no
coverage count is stated anywhere (W2). PSG §11.6.1 deliberately unchanged.

### D3 — `chat-hierarchy.md:201-205` reconciled ✅

The corollary no longer rests default-accept on *"the human's key is present at the
session by construction."* It now states that acceptance is carried by an acknowledgment
that **names the party that reviewed and accepted** (role + session identity), that
**silence accepts nothing**, and that presence is not evidence of review — the
acknowledgment is. The manual/agentic line survives: an agentic instance's silence is not
an acknowledgment and does not by itself accept a delivery. Version 1.3.0 → **1.4.0**.

### D4 — What the signal does NOT claim ✅

Stated normatively in PSG §11.6: the acknowledgment distinguishes **review-happened** from
**nobody-looked**; it does **not** make the review **good**. *"A review happened"* is not
*"the review was correct"* — the boundary is stated, not implied (E39.3's overclaim,
refused).
