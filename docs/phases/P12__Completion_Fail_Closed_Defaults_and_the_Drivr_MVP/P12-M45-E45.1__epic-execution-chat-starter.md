> **This milestone builds the thing that decides whether other things worked. It must not be trusted on
> its own report.**

- **State the layer.** `Completion` or `Reading` — every claim names which, per Y3.

  deliverable that does not say where it lands cannot be verified.

  runs and neither by a replay. **A replay suite is a regression guard, not a discovery instrument**, and
  you must not report replay-only evidence as though it discovered anything.

- **Pin the ref, and record it.** A number without a ref is not a measurement.
- **`undetermined` is the answer when the evidence is absent. It is never a synonym for "no".**

**The five things easiest to get wrong, flagged here and specified there:**

1. **Committing anything before the bar.** The bar is the **first commit on the branch** — shown by
   `git log`, not asserted in the Delivery Notice.
2. **Changing Drivr judgment code.** `completion.py`, `projections.py`, `_decide` — **do not touch.**
   A bar that changes the thing it measures has failed its only job.
3. **A shorter evidence set than declared.** An unloadable case is a loud, named failure — never a
   quietly shortened list (E41.2 S3).
4. **Penalising `undetermined` on absent evidence.** It scores **correct**; a rule that penalises honest
   uncertainty trains the signal to guess.
5. **Reasoning about a state name without reading the enum** (Y5). `Completion.UNDETERMINED` does not
   exist; `Reading.UNDETERMINED` does.
