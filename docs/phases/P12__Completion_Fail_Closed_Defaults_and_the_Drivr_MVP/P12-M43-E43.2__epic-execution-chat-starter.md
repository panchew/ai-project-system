## Summary

**Replace silence as the sole carrier of acceptance with a cheap positive signal.**

W3 narrows the live gap to the **manual** case — agentic silence already accepts nothing
(`chat-hierarchy.md:201-205`). The manual case rests on an **attendance presumption**, *"the human's
key is present by construction,"* which is not evidence of review. The milestone spec's fourth state
(duplicated role) and auditability property require that whatever replaces silence be **a signal an
identified party emitted**, never an absence attributed to a role. **The design must keep default-accept's
cheapness**: a clean delivery still costs no new artifact, so the signal rides the §11.6 acknowledgment
that already exists — made to carry the party that reviewed.

   duplication leaves **evidence**, nothing more.

   refused).
5. **Reconcile, do not stack.** `chat-hierarchy.md:201-205` and the amended §11.6 must agree; the
   manual/agentic line survives, and the manual case stops resting on attendance.
