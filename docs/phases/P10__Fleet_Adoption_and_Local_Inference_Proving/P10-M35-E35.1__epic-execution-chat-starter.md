### Summary

The framework has a machine-wide **request-executing desk** (System HQ, canonized in P9-M32). It does
not have a written **fleet operator** — the role that runs the serialized local-inference lane,
sequences what runs next, and keeps registered projects current on governance version. The CFO has
been performing that role by hand through M33 and M34 with no record of what it may or may never do.
You are writing the role down, form-neutral, with its authority boundary explicit.

### Problem Statement

Three facts:
- **The role is real and unwritten.** M33 and M34 ran with the CFO as the lane. Nothing in the
  governance corpus says what the operator may do.
- **The seam is what makes writing it safe.** "Update every project to v7.0.0" is a fleet-wide write.
  It must not execute on a spoken word — the same rule that governs the Creation Chat. An operator
  recorded without that seam recreates, one level down, the exact thing the framework exists to
  prevent.
- **The form was wrong in M35's original scoping.** SN-24 caught it: the operator was described as a
  chat. HQ ruled the record must name the **role**, and reference the daemon **once**. Governance
  names the role; P11 names the thing that runs it.
