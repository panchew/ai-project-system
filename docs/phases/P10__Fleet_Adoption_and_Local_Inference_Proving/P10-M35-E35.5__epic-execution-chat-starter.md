## The blinding discipline (read twice)

**Every one of the five defects is documented in this repository, usually with the finding and its fix
stated plainly.** A local model pointed at the repo and asked to review will *read* the answer, not
derive it — and the result will look like a pass while measuring nothing.

- **Build each packet from the material as it stood at the moment of the decision** — what a Stage-2
  reviewer actually had in hand.
- **Exclude every document that states the finding**, its fix, or references either. The spec's
  defects table names what to excise per defect.
- **Commit the packets**, so a reader can verify the blinding rather than trust your account of it.
- **Pre-register the rubric** — catch / miss / false alarm per defect — and **commit it before any
  run**. Git history must show the rubric predates the results. A rubric written after seeing outputs
  is a rationalization.
- **Report every run you make.** Non-determinism makes repeat runs legitimate; silent best-of-N
  selection falsifies the exact property being measured.

**Two directions are being measured, deliberately.** Defects 2 and 5 require flagging something that
looked fine (a green exit; a red test). Defect 3 requires *not* condemning work that was correct. A
model that flags everything passes two and fails one — the rubric must separate a genuine catch from
indiscriminate alarm, and **false alarms are recorded, not ignored.**
