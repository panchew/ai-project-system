### Summary

The operator's role and boundary are recorded (E35.1); what a blocked instance owes is recorded
(E35.3). Nothing says what the operator needs to **know** to do the job on a given cycle. You are
writing that: a **standing brief** — form-neutral, consumable by a chat re-reading it, a daemon
loading it on boot, or a person consulting it, on whatever cadence that filler has.

### Problem Statement

- **SN-22 named the gap**; E32.2 answered it for **System HQ** with a seed. The fleet operator has no
  equivalent.
- **SN-24 retired the form, not the content.** "Daily re-instantiation" presumes a chat re-spawning
  itself each morning. HQ struck that framing: the operator is not a chat. What survives is a
  standing brief with no cadence of its own.
- **The repository does not know the fleet's state.** Enrollment, live versions, and lane contents are
  machine-local (`~/.ai-project/SYSTEM-GOVERNANCE.md`, the read-only MCP bridge). A brief that writes
  them down is wrong the week after it is merged.
