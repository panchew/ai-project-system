### Summary

Record a Steering Note ID allocation rule and the *bookkeeping-never-rewrites-a-normative-citation*
separating rule, then date-qualify every SN-23 citation in the normative tier so that no reader can
follow a citation and conclude platform agnosticism was superseded.

### Problem Statement

`AI-OPERATING-GUIDELINES.md:124` and `chat-hierarchy.md:165` both cite **"SN-23 Decision 2"** meaning
entirely unrelated decisions, and the latter declares its one **superseded**. A reader following the
AOG citation by number lands on the supersession notice and concludes **platform agnosticism was
superseded** — it was not. Underneath that, ID allocation has **no recorded rule**, so a future
author has no way to determine the next ID except by inferring precedent from prior notes — the exact
process that produced the two existing collisions.

### Goals

1. A future note author can determine the correct next ID **from the recorded rule alone.**
2. The separating rule — *a bookkeeping defect never rewrites a citation in a normative document* —
   is recorded normatively, making M36's asymmetric treatment of the two collisions coherent.
3. **No un-dated SN-23 citation remains in the normative tier**, and the trap is closed.
4. The record states explicitly that **SN-23 is not renumbered, and why.**
