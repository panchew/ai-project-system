### The restatement-vs-mention distinction, stated and applied

- **A restatement** — the text **asserts** the Seed lacks the check → **amend.**
- **A mere mention** — names the item without asserting its premise → **leave.**
- **Already carries the re-diagnosis** → **leave.**
