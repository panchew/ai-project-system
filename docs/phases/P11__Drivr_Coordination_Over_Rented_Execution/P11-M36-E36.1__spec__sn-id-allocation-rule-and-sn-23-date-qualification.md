# Epic E36.1 — Steering Note ID allocation rule + SN-23 date-qualified citations

## Context

Milestone M36 (*Record Integrity and Documentation Hygiene*) is P11's first milestone by CFO ruling
(2026-08-01, recorded in SN-28). It lands four self-contained documentation items **governed** —
with a spec, a Definition of Done, a Stage-2 review and a closure record — **before any Drivr code
exists.**

This Epic is the milestone's foundation in a literal sense. Constraint 2 of the milestone spec makes
the allocation rule land **before anything is renumbered**, so that E36.2's rename applies a
*recorded* rule rather than inventing one. Nothing in M36 renumbers until this Epic's rule is on the
record.

It also closes the milestone's only **High-severity** item, and that item is not the duplication.
`governance/AI-OPERATING-GUIDELINES.md` and `governance/systems/chat-hierarchy.md` **both cite
"SN-23 Decision 2" meaning entirely unrelated decisions**, and `chat-hierarchy.md` declares its one
**superseded**. Verified on `milestone/M36` at planning time:

| Location | Text | Which note it means |
|---|---|---|
| `AI-OPERATING-GUIDELINES.md:124` | "Fallback — repo-less delivery (**SN-23 Decision 2, ratified**)" | SN-23 **(2026-07-18)** — reference-first / platform agnosticism |
| `chat-hierarchy.md:165` | "**SN-23 Ratified Decision #2 is superseded** on the Execution Mode axis only" | SN-23 **(2026-07-20)** — the P10 adoption spine |

A reader following the AOG citation by number lands on the supersession notice and concludes
**platform agnosticism was superseded.** It was not. A *different* Decision 2 was, on a *different*
axis, in a *different* note. **That is the trap this Epic closes** — and an Epic that treats it as
tidy-up will under-scope the sweep.

The root cause is that ID allocation had **no enforcement of any kind** — no registry, no rule, no
test until bugfix B3.1 (merged `65f83fe`, 2026-08-02). That works exactly as well as the author's
attention, and both collisions are what it looks like when attention lapses.

---

## Problem Statement

Three distinct defects, all in this framework's own record:

1. **No allocation rule exists.** A future note author has no recorded way to determine the correct
   next ID. They must read prior notes and infer precedent — the exact process that produced two
   collisions. HQ Ruling 2026-08-01 Decision 3 **answers** the underlying namespace question (one
   sequence per directory, regardless of issuing entity), but that answer lives in a ruling, not in
   the surfaces a note author actually reads.

2. **No rule explains why one collision is renumbered and the other is not.** M36 renumbers `SN-1`
   (E36.2) and deliberately leaves `SN-23` colliding (Decision 4). Without a recorded separating
   principle, that reads as arbitrary, and a future reader "fixing" the remaining collision would
   invalidate every citation this Epic corrects.

3. **The SN-23 citations are ambiguous by number throughout the normative tier** — the High-severity
   trap above.

If this Epic is not completed: E36.2 cannot begin (constraint 2), the trap stays open, and the
recurrence surface stays exactly as unenforced as it was.

---

## Goals

By the end of this Epic:

1. **A future note author can determine the correct next Steering Note ID from the recorded rule
   alone**, without reading prior notes for precedent.
2. **The separating rule — *a bookkeeping defect never rewrites a citation in a normative
   document* — is recorded normatively**, making M36's asymmetric treatment of the two collisions
   coherent rather than arbitrary.
3. **No un-dated SN-23 citation remains in the normative tier**, and no reader can reach "platform
   agnosticism was superseded" by following a citation.
4. **The record states explicitly that SN-23 is not renumbered, and why**, so the remaining
   collision is legible as a ratified decision rather than as unfinished cleanup.

---

## Non-Goals

This Epic explicitly does **not** aim to:

- **Re-derive the namespace question.** It is answered — one sequence per steering-note directory,
  regardless of issuing entity (HQ Ruling 2026-08-01, Decision 3). **Applying it is the work;
  re-deciding it is not.**
- **Renumber anything.** No renumbering happens in this Epic. `SN-1`'s rename is E36.2's, and
  **no epic renumbers anything on its own initiative** (constraint 2).
- **Renumber SN-23.** Ruled 2026-08-01, Decision 4. Citations carry the date instead.
- **Build a registry, a validator, or a linter.** M36 amends the record; it builds no mechanism
  (Hard Constraint). B3.1's test already exists and is E36.2's obligation, not this Epic's.
- **Touch `tests/test_steering_note_id_uniqueness.py`.** That file is E36.2's, exclusively
  (constraint 2a). This Epic leaves the suite's xfail exactly as it finds it.
- **Fold in P10-GH-8** (`governance/systems/` versions and changelogs inconsistent). Parked; the
  Phase Chat recommended against folding it into M36 and HQ has not directed otherwise. **If HQ
  directs it, it lands here — but only on an explicit HQ direction, not on this Epic's initiative.**
- **Audit artifact families beyond steering notes.** That is E36.5's, and it reports rather than
  fixes.

## Binding Constraints (reproduced — not merely cited)

These are settled and **not for re-debate.** They are reproduced here in full because an Epic
session must not have to fetch them.

**1. The namespace question is ANSWERED. Do not re-derive it.**
HQ Ruling 2026-08-01, Decision 3: **one sequence per steering-note directory, regardless of issuing
entity.** A note filed into a project's `steering-notes/` takes the next free `SN-<n>`; sub-IDs keep
letter suffixes (`SN-12a`). Provenance is already recorded in `issuer_chat` and the filename slug;
**the identifier names position and nothing else.**

**2. E36.1 lands before E36.2.** The rule is applied before anything is renumbered. **This Epic
renumbers nothing.**

**3. SN-23 is NOT renumbered.** Citations carry the date: `SN-23 (2026-07-18)` = reference-first /
platform agnosticism; `SN-23 (2026-07-20)` = the P10 adoption spine. The separating rule — *a
bookkeeping defect never rewrites a citation in a normative document* — is normative and **must be
recorded**.

**8. Every M36 delivery that amends a normative document carries a Structural diagram** (Mermaid,
fenced, in-repo, **no ComfyUI**) per `governance/systems/hq-chat.md` "Review Diagram on HQ Rulings" —
documents touched, what changed named to the section, what was deliberately frozen, where authority
flowed. **This is what makes the CFO's §11.6.1 diff review cheap enough to actually perform.** This
Epic amends the normative tier, so **the obligation fires.**

**Hard Constraint (milestone-wide).** **M36 amends the record. It builds no mechanism.** If this Epic
finds itself building a registry, a validator, a linter, or any new enforcement, **it has drifted out
of scope and must stop and escalate to the Milestone Chat rather than proceed.**

## Scope of Work

### 1. The allocation rule, recorded normatively

Record the rule in **both** `governance/templates/steering-note.md` **and**
`governance/systems/creation-chat-guide.md`.

**Must include:**
- The next ID is the **highest existing ID in the directory plus one, regardless of issuing entity.**
- Sub-IDs keep the existing letter-suffix form (`SN-12a`, `SN-12b`) and are distinct IDs, not
  collisions.
- **The reason stated alongside the rule** — provenance is already recorded in `issuer_chat` and the
  filename slug, so **the identifier names position and nothing else.** A rule whose reason is
  recorded survives an edit that a bare rule does not.
- A pointer to B3.1's guard (`tests/test_steering_note_id_uniqueness.py`) as the mechanical check, so
  an author knows the rule is enforced rather than merely stated.

### 2. The separating rule, recorded normatively

Record: ***a bookkeeping defect never rewrites a citation in a normative document.***

**Must include:**
- Where a colliding ID is cited **only in project-internal, non-normative artifacts** → **renumber.**
- Where it is cited in the **normative tier** → **date-qualify the citations** and leave the
  collision visible rather than laundered.
- The statement that this is what makes renumbering `SN-1` and *not* `SN-23` coherent instead of
  arbitrary.

> **Which surface holds it is this Epic's design decision.** `creation-chat-guide.md`, alongside the
> allocation rule, is the natural candidate — a reader who has just learned how IDs are allocated is
> the reader who needs to know what happens when allocation went wrong. **Decide, record the
> reasoning, and proceed. Do not escalate this.**

Date-qualify every SN-23 citation in the normative tier: `SN-23 (2026-07-18)` for reference-first /
platform agnosticism, `SN-23 (2026-07-20)` for the P10 adoption spine.

**Three refinements the sweep must handle deliberately, each verified at planning time:**

- **Already-path-qualified citations.** `fleet-operator.md:220` and `fleet-operator-brief.md:274`
  already disambiguate **by file path**, not by date. They are not ambiguous to a careful reader.
  **The Epic decides whether path-qualification satisfies the goal or whether the date form is added
  for consistency** — and records which, with its reasoning. Either is defensible; silence is not.
- **`creation-chat-guide.md:150` is genuinely ambiguous.** "it is how SN-23, SN-24 and SN-25
  themselves arrived" is a statement about the *channel*, listing notes as artifacts. Both SN-23s are
  Creation Chat notes, so neither reading is obviously wrong. **The Epic decides and records.**
- **SN-27's citation is half-done.** The milestone spec names "SN-27's own Ratified Decision #7
  citation" as in scope. Verified: `2026-07-31__creation-chat__steering-note__P11-drivr-spine.md`
  **line 27 is already date-qualified** (`SN-23 (2026-07-20) — Ratified Decision #7`); **line 101 is
  not** ("the form of SN-23 Ratified Decision #7"). **Line 101 is the one that needs work.**

> **The inventory is a floor, not a guarantee.** SN-28's audit table listed *representative*
> citations. **The Epic performs its own exhaustive sweep** — `grep -rn "SN-23"` over `governance/`
> **and** `.ai-project/artifacts/` at minimum — treats every occurrence deliberately, and **states
> where its findings differ from this table.** Line numbers here are planning-time facts, not
> guarantees.

**Changelog lines are a judgment call this Epic must make explicitly, not silently.** A changelog
entry is a historical record of what an edit said at the time. Date-qualifying it may be correct (a
reader follows it like any other citation) or may be falsifying the record. **The Epic decides,
records the rule it applied, and applies it consistently.** Either outcome is acceptable; an unstated
one is not. This is one of the three design decisions the milestone spec assigns to the Epic Chat —
**decide it; do not escalate it.**

### 4. The explicit "SN-23 is not renumbered, and why" statement

Record it where a future reader hits it — so nobody "fixes" the remaining collision and invalidates
every citation this Epic just corrected.

**Must include:**
- The statement that both notes keep `id: SN-23` **permanently, by decision** (HQ Ruling 2026-08-01,
  Decision 4).
- Its reason: the ID is cited in the normative tier, so the separating rule sends it to
  *date-qualify*, not to *renumber*.
- The interaction with B3.1's guard: the `SN-23` collision is a **ratified, allowlisted exception**,
  not an outstanding defect. (The allowlist itself is E36.2's to build — this Epic states the
  ratification; it does not touch the test.)

### 5. Structural diagram

A Mermaid diagram, fenced, in-repo, **no ComfyUI**, per constraint 8. It must name: documents
touched, what changed **named to the section**, what was deliberately frozen (SN-23's ID; the
`SN-1` rename deferred to E36.2; the test file untouched), and where authority flowed (**nowhere
new** — this Epic executes ruled decisions).
