## Your design decision — the stack — and its five constraints

**Drivr's stack is yours. Pick it, document the reasoning, proceed. Do not escalate it.**

The constraints are **SN-24's headless-first** and *"as agentic as we can → lightest infrastructure"* —
**not a named technology.** What the choice must satisfy:

1. **Invoke a CLI tool as a subprocess** and read its exit code, stdout, and stderr. That is the
   entirety of what E38.2's adapter surface needs from the runtime.
2. **Read YAML** — E38.3's registry reads `.ai-project.yml` across the fleet.
3. **Be testable** — your baseline is a deliverable and a baseline of zero measures nothing.
4. **Be headless** — no GUI, no window, no interactive-only entry point. The thin surface is M40's.
5. **Pull in no inference SDK, model client, or agent framework.** **Constraint 1 is enforceable at the
   dependency list, and that is the cheapest place to enforce it.**

**Lightest thing that satisfies all five wins.** If two candidates both satisfy them, take the one with
fewer dependencies and record that as the tiebreak. **Write the reasoning down before you create
anything** — it is easier to write honestly before the code exists.
