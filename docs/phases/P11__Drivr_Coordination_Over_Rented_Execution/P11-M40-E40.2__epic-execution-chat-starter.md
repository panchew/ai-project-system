### Summary

Build a **gate queue computed from governance state**, never hand-maintained; **demonstrate its
derivation by recomputation**; **state what it reads**; and make **unclassifiable artifacts surface
rather than vanish.** State coverage as a **floor, with a count and a date.**

### The one thing to understand before designing: silent omission

**Both natural implementation shortcuts were measured against this repository and both drop the
majority of the corpus without raising anything:**

| Shortcut | What it actually sees |
|---|---|
| Build on `SCHEMA_REGISTRY` | **3 of 19 families** — 155 of 203 artifacts (76%) omitted. An unregistered type is not an error; it is simply not a hit. **`merge-authorizations` and `escalation-notices` are both gate-bearing and neither is registered.** |
| Read one root | **5 of 104 delivery notices (5%)** — the corpus is bi-located and 99 delivery notices live under `docs/`. |

**A queue that is wrong loudly costs a human ten seconds. A queue that is wrong quietly costs a
gate** — and a human cannot audit a list for what is not on it. **Spec §D3–§D5 exist because of
this.**

**Full schema coverage of 19 families is almost certainly too large for this epic.** A coarser
recognizer that classifies more and validates less is a legitimate answer — **provided the coverage
number is honest about it** (spec §D5, delegated decision 2).
