### Summary

Turn M23's binding *mechanism* into routine *behavior*: establish, in AOG §16, that every level
produces **both** a *proposed* visual (before build) and an *implemented* visual (after) as the
routine default when the capability is enabled — **"nothing is too much"**, **Structural-first** —
and extend the guide's per-level worked examples (§5) to show a proposed and an implemented example
at each level, each recorded via the existing §7 binding's `State` field. **Documentation only** —
no code, no helper change, no schema change.

### Problem Statement

M23 gave every level a §7 home for a link + metadata, with a `State` field that *can* hold `proposed`
or `implemented`. But nothing tells an agent that producing **both** is the routine default, how
generous to be, or that the cheap Structural path should carry most coverage. The two-track signal
goes unused and the "nothing is too much" bar has no operative instruction. This Epic makes the
two-track behavior the documented default and shows, per level, what proposed and implemented look
like.

### Goals

1. **Establish the two-track expectation in AOG §16** — a new subsection directing every level to
   produce both a *proposed* and an *implemented* visual as the routine default when
   `visual_artifacts.enabled: true`, with the **"nothing is too much"** bar and **Structural-first**
   preference.
2. **Show per-level proposed/implemented examples in guide §5** — for Creation / HQ / Phase /
   Milestone / Epic, each recorded as a §7 binding via the `State` field.
3. **Reuse the §7 schema, never restate it** — reference the binding schema documented once in §7; do
   not redefine the five-element block or the `State` field, and do not regress by-link.
4. **Record the behavior** (AOG Changelog, minor bump → v2.4.0; guide Changelog) and **keep the suite
   green**.

## Required Content — what the behavior must encode

**AOG §16 new subsection (e.g. §16.6 "Proposed vs. implemented" — exact number is your design call
within M24's scope):**

- The **two-track expectation as routine default**: when enabled, every level produces **both** a
  *proposed* visual (intent, before build) and an *implemented* visual (what was built, after) — not
  one or the other.
- The **"nothing is too much" coverage bar** (be generous; err toward producing the pair).
- The **Structural-first preference** as the reason it's affordable: most coverage is free
  Mermaid/PlantUML text (no endpoint, no cost); reserve Generative for where a render communicates
  better.
- A pointer that the two tracks are recorded via the **§7 binding's `State` field**
  (`proposed` / `implemented`) — **reference** guide §7, do not restate the schema; a level may carry
  one binding of each `State`.
- Respect the gates: applies **only when enabled** (§16.1); a tool-less surface produces Structural
  only and **defers** generative intent per §16.4 rather than fabricating it.
- *Optional, surgical:* a single sentence in §16.2 and/or §16.3 tying the per-level abstraction and
  the Structural-first preference to the two tracks. Do not restructure those subsections.

**Guide §5 per-level examples (Creation / HQ / Phase / Milestone / Epic):**

- For each level, a *proposed* example and an *implemented* example, consistent with that level's
  abstraction (the SN-11 table) and Structural-first where applicable.
- Each example recorded as a **§7 binding** with the appropriate `State`, using a **hosted link** for
  any generated visual (never a committed path). The binding **reuses** the §7 block (points to §7),
  it does not re-document it.
- Demonstrate the Structural-first economy: most pairs are two text diagrams; show a Generative pair
  (e.g. Creation concept, Epic mockup) only where a render is warranted.
- This is an **extension** of §5 (proposed + implemented per level), not a rewrite of its structure.

**Changelogs:**

  v2.4.0 two-track), citing E24.1 (P6-M24).
