## Execution Summary

Composition was measured before anything was cut (o200k proxy, G10 ±10–15%
throughout). The measurements **corrected the spec's lever hypothesis**: verbatim
starter↔spec duplication is only ~9% of each M30 epic starter (273–323 tokens;
starters paraphrase) — the dominant reducible component is **full PSG+AOG loaded
at every level** (22,523 tokens = 62–77% of every pack), much of it
role-irrelevant or dormant (changelogs 2,844; AOG §3 non-own chat-type
subsections; AOG §16 2,259; PSG §5B/§5C/§8A/§14C ≈ 4,594). Structural (template-
mandated) starter↔spec duplication is nonetheless real at the epic level:
905–1,120 tokens per M30 starter of Deliverables/DoD/AC/Technical-Constraints
copies plus an embedded DN template.

Levers chosen per level, from the composition table (standard §8, DD2): PSG/AOG
**section scoping** (core / conditional-on-trigger / don't-load) at all three
levels; **template dedup** (load-one-reference-the-other) at the epic level only,
where duplication was measured; **parent-spec targeting** at the milestone level
(phase spec by section: ≤912 vs 5,268). No dedup was claimed for
milestone/phase starters — measured 0% verbatim duplication (honesty over
magnitude).

**Before/after pack numbers (E30.1 mechanism, Direction B, G10 error bars):**
epic 29,336 → 12,005 (−59%); milestone 36,614 → 15,971 (−56%); phase
30,478 → 14,586 (−52%). Component arithmetic and reproduction method are
committed in the standard (§4.2, §7). Billed per-call medians (dataset §3) are
**not** claimed to have moved — billed improvement is forward-looking, to be
verified by a future mechanism capture (recommended to M31).

**Scoping-standard home:**
`.ai-project/artifacts/reference/token-measurement/context-scoping.md` (DD1 —
keeps the E30.1→E30.3 evidence chain in one directory; templates carry
self-contained load lists so `governance/` stays portable to adopters).

**Bounded-scope finding headline (standard §5):** even perfect pack scoping
leaves ≥60–80% of per-call context untouched — history, tool results, harness
overhead — and the dominant lever is behavioral: parent-chat session length and
mixing (G7; 53% of window spend). Handed to M31 (one-task-one-session
discipline; rerun-and-compare verification) and the Phase Chat (segmentation
remains future measurement work; E30.4 owns the mandated-echo half).

**Extent vs the spec's Sizing Decision: match.** Bounded middle path delivered —
governance-pack slice implemented with measured before/after; non-governance
share documented as findings, not work. One recorded correction inside that
extent: the lever mix shifted from the spec's dedup-first hypothesis to
section-scoping-first, because the numbers said so (standard §2.3, §8 DD2) —
this is the measured-first discipline operating, not a deviation from the
sizing decision.
