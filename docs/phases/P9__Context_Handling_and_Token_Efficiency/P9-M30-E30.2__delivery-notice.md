**Headline audit findings** (all price-weighted, every claim cited to a
dataset cell or G-id):

- The measured window (72 sessions, 35 days) cost **≈ $623**. Cache reads are
  94.8% of raw volume but only **48.5% of cost** after weighting;
  cache-creation (29.8%) and output (21.4%) carry the other half — the
  misstatement raw-count audits would make is quantified in report §2.1.
- **The parent chats are the biggest sink, not the epic executors:**
  milestone 37.0% + phase 16.1% of spend, dominated by long mixed sessions
  (G7; median $11.71–$20.84/session) vs epic execution 23.1% (38 sessions,
  median $2.92).
- **The stale `models:` names were fiction:** neither `gpt-4o` nor
  `claude-3-5-sonnet` appears in any of the 72 sessions (dataset §2);
  `claude-opus-4-8` carried 49.6% of spend and the plurality at hq, phase,
  and milestone.
- Needed-frontier vs local-capable (judgment, labeled, gap-cited): epic ×
  execution is the designated local-offload experiment by shape but unproven
  (G9 + new G11/G12); all judgment-bearing levels stay paid frontier;
  **context-load reduction (E30.3) outranks model downgrade as a cost lever**.
- Corpus overhead: per-level governance packs 29–37K proxy tokens vs billed
  per-call context medians of 76–169K — governance corpus is a material
  minority of context load; E30.3 must target total per-call context.
