# Delivery Notice: P9-M32-E32.2 — System Chat re-instantiation seed

## Summary

Built System HQ's re-instantiation seed (`governance/systems/system-hq-seed.md`) — a
self-contained, repeatable daily-spawn prompt the CFO can paste into a fresh session to
reconstruct System HQ's identity and constraints with no other document required. It is
analogous in mechanic to `seed.md`'s "Rule 5 — Re-instantiation" but adapted to System HQ's

no reconciliation is owed.

## Design Decisions (resolved, with reasoning)

**Design Decision 1 — Form and home: (B) a system-tier document, at
`governance/systems/system-hq-seed.md`.** E32.1 shipped `governance/systems/system-hq.md` and
spent its own two design decisions establishing that System HQ is out-of-hierarchy and *not*
per-project (contrast table, explicit "not Level 5", annex placed away from the numbered
levels). Placing its seed inside `governance/templates/` — otherwise entirely per-project
scaffolding — would have structurally implied the opposite of the thing E32.1 just
established. Option B's stated con in the spec ("a genuinely new kind of location, no
existing precedent") no longer applied at execution time: E32.1 created that precedent, and
the seed now sits directly adjacent to the document it references, which is where a reader
who found one will look for the other. Structure follows `seed.md` deliberately, so the
pattern reads familiarly by analogy even though the location differs.

**Design Decision 2 — What the seed says, minimally.** Identity and cross-project scope;
the Authority Boundary; seven Rules of Engagement (execute-never-decide; mandatory
`status: escalated`; artifacts-not-chat + immutability; outward-facing actions need human
confirmation; the framework is governed by its own process; stay in scope — the deferred
MCP write path and scheduled sweep named as *not yours to build*; re-instantiation);
discovery via the read-only bridge; and how to answer.

The division between reproduction and reference was the substantive call:

- **Authority Boundary — reproduced verbatim.** Self-containment (the seed must work alone)
  and the "word-for-word agreement" acceptance criterion both point the same way, and it is
  the technique E32.1 itself used for the `chat-hierarchy.md` annex. Verified with `diff`:
  **byte-identical** to `system-hq.md`'s canonical block, with `system-hq.md` explicitly
  named authoritative on divergence.
- **Schemas, storage paths, naming, status vocabulary — referenced, never restated.** These
  are operational detail, not identity; duplicating them would create exactly the divergence
  risk the Epic exists to avoid. The seed instructs its reader to open `system-hq.md` before
  writing a first artifact, "rather than working from memory, and never from a paraphrase."

**Model verification section — included, stating that no expectation exists.** The spec
required saying so rather than silently omitting. `.ai-project.yml`'s `models:` keys (`hq`,
`phase`, `milestone`, `epic_dev`, `epic_qa`, `creation`, `epic_manual`) are all per-project;
System HQ is not among them. The seed therefore instructs the agent to state plainly at
startup that no model expectation is configured, warns explicitly against adopting a
project's `models.hq` as its own, and marks where a STOP-on-mismatch check would go if a
machine-level expectation is ever introduced.

## Authority confirmation (explicit, per Deliverable 3)

**Nothing in this seed grants System HQ review, merge, or scope authority.** The seed states
so in its own words directly under the Authority Boundary — "This seed grants you no
authority whatsoever beyond that boundary… not by statement, not by omission, and not by
implication from any instruction below" — and Rule 2 makes `status: escalated` mandatory for
anything review-, merge-, or scope-shaped, judged **by shape, not by wording**, with
pre-authorizing language in a request explicitly called out as not changing the shape. No
section describing what System HQ may do is silent on execute-never-decide (Hard
Constraint 1).
