# Delivery Notice: P9-M32-E32.1 — SN-21 canonization

## Summary

Canonized System HQ — the cross-project, one-desk-per-machine system participant adopted
in the field on 2026-07-16 (SN-21) — into governance. The `system_request`/`system_response`
schemas, storage/naming conventions, status vocabulary, and a normative execute-never-decide
authority boundary now live in a new companion document, cross-referenced from the Artifact
Communication Protocol and recorded as an explicit out-of-hierarchy annex in the chat
hierarchy. All conventions were taken from the field record; none were invented.

## Deliverables

- ✓ **`governance/systems/system-hq.md`** (new) — schema home (Design Decision 1B). Contains
  the `system_request` and `system_response` schemas verbatim from SN-21 field usage
  (storage paths `.ai-project/artifacts/system-{requests,responses}/`, the
  `<ISO-timestamp>__<project>__<type>.md` naming convention, and the
  `pending | in_progress | done | declined | escalated` status vocabulary), a structural
  "not a fifth level" contrast table, and the **canonical** normative Authority Boundary.
- ✓ **`governance/systems/artifact-communication-protocol.md`** (v1.3.0 → v1.4.0) — one
  `## Reference` line pointing to the companion doc, plus a changelog row. `## Artifact
  Types`, the two existing schemas, the Communication Flow Diagram, and all prior changelog
  rows are untouched.
- ✓ **`governance/systems/chat-hierarchy.md`** — an early pointer note under "The Four-Level
  Chat Hierarchy" and a new out-of-hierarchy annex ("System HQ — Out-of-Hierarchy,
  Cross-Project Participant") placed near the end, away from the numbered-level material
  (Design Decision 2). Every existing section (Execution Mode, Manual Chat Model
  Verification, Working-Tree Isolation, Scope Direction Protocol, Communication Protocol,
  Authorization Artifacts) is preserved unreworked.
- ✓ Full test suite: **363 passed**, no regressions, no new skips.

## Design Decisions (resolved, with reasoning)

**Design Decision 1 — Schema home: (B) companion document.** The protocol's existing types
(Delivery Notice, Review Decision) are all *intra-project*, and its Core Principles and
Communication Flow Diagram assume a single-project chain — both forbidden to edit, and both
would sit in tension with an *inter-project* pair added inside `## Artifact Types`. A
companion document (`system-hq.md`) keeps the cross-project pair coherent, gives it the
canonical home for the Authority Boundary that both surfaces reference, and leaves room for
E32.2's seed to point at rather than restate. The protocol remains discoverable via its
`## Reference` section.

**Design Decision 2 — Hierarchy placement: out-of-hierarchy annex.** Recorded in
`chat-hierarchy.md` as a distinctly-titled annex placed **away** from the four-level
material (a placement adjacent to "The Four-Level Chat Hierarchy" risks implying inclusion
in it — the spec's stated hazard). An early pointer up top ensures a reader hits it, while a
structural contrast table and explicit "not a fifth level / different axis" language — not a
single prose disclaimer — carry the cross-project, one-desk-per-machine distinction.

## Authority-boundary consistency

The Authority Boundary is **canonical in `system-hq.md`** and **reproduced verbatim** in the
`chat-hierarchy.md` annex. The two blocks were diffed and are byte-identical; each names the
other, with `system-hq.md` designated authoritative on any divergence. It is locatable from
both surfaces (schema home and hierarchy-placement location) and is silent nowhere on
execute-never-decide.
