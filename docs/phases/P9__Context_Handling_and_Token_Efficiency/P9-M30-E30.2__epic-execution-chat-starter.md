### Binding constraints (carry into every decision)

**Hard Constraint (verbatim from the Milestone spec):**

> **The frontier-vs-local policy and the `models:` refresh MUST be derived from the
> captured measurements — not from pre-existing assumptions.** Real captured data is the
> bar; estimates do not count (phase acceptance criterion). If a measurement cannot be
> captured for some level or task type, **record the gap explicitly** in the report
> rather than substituting a guess — a policy row grounded in a recorded gap is
> acceptable; a policy row grounded in an unlabeled assumption is not. This constraint
> governs E30.2 directly and sizes E30.3: no reduction work may be scoped from the prior
> 24K/157K estimates alone.

- **Every report claim and policy row cites its dataset cell (section/row) or G-id.**
  Lower-confidence rows (branch-fallback, mixed buckets, one-run local evidence) say so.
- The prior 24K/157K estimates are history only — dataset §4 has the measured corpus
  numbers; use those.
- **Price weighting documented** wherever volumes become cost (cache-read ≈0.1×,
  cache-creation ≈1.25×, output at its own rate — state your rates and source).
- **Only `models:` changes in `.ai-project.yml`** — no other field.
- No new measurement capture; unanswerable questions become new numbered gap records.

### Design Decisions (yours to resolve — decide, document, move; do not escalate to ask)

1. **Policy documentation home** — governance doc, yml-spec section, or both. Document
   the choice + reasoning in the report; cross-link home ↔ report ↔ `models:` block.
2. **Refresh shape** — pure value refresh (same five keys: no yml-spec bump) vs semantics
   change (new keys such as `creation`, per-task routing, fallback entries: bump +
   changelog + §3.4 table + validation rules 14–17 updated coherently). Follow the
   evidence; don't contort the policy to avoid a bump, don't bump for values.
3. **yml-spec §3.4 stale defaults** — even on a pure value refresh, §3.4 documents
   `remote:gpt-4o`/`remote:claude-3-5-sonnet` as defaults. Update them (bump +
   changelog) or record why they stand (format illustrations superseded by the recorded
   policy — say where). Silence is not an option; state the decision in the report.
4. **Report location** — recommended
   `.ai-project/artifacts/reference/token-measurement/audit-report.md` (beside its
   evidence); your final call, documented and stable.
