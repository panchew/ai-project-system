# Review Decision: P11-M38-E38.3 — Fleet Registry, §4 Validator, and `framework_version`

The implementation is accepted. The validator is read-only, structurally parses YAML, preserves the
normative warning/error distinction, fails on genuinely invalid configs, appends rule 26 without
renumbering rules 1–25, and carries 95 new test cases in the delivered suite. Constraint 9 fired and
the required Structural diagram is present. Re-measured suite: **488 passed**.

Two record-integrity defects remain. Correct only these; do not reopen implementation, classification,
the P10-GH-1 fold-in, the self-referential exemption, or any escalation.

## Required rework

### 1. Separate raw-run warning totals from fleet warning totals

`.ai-project/registry/fleet-registry.yml` records:

```yaml
validation:
  invalid_configs: 4
  total_errors: 8
  total_warnings: 14
```

That block otherwise describes **13 enrolled fleet projects**, but `14` is the raw `--fleet` run's
warning total over **15 configs**, including two `ai-project-system` worktrees. Excluding those two
one-warning worktrees, the actual fleet total is **12 warnings**.

This is the same scope contamination the Epic correctly identified for the config count, left active
in a neighboring aggregate. Fix the registry and Delivery Notice so both scopes are explicit and
cannot be conflated. A sufficient shape is:

```yaml
validation:
  fleet_configs: 13
  fleet_total_warnings: 12
  raw_configs_checked: 15
  raw_total_warnings: 14
  excluded_worktree_configs: 2
```

Equivalent naming is acceptable. Add or extend a registry invariant test so the fleet warning total
is derived from the 15 project entries and the raw/fleet distinction cannot silently collapse again.

### 2. Convert the state-definition discrepancy from present tense to resolved history

The Epic correctly found that the Starter claimed **verbatim** while carrying a compressed paraphrase.
The Milestone Chat has corrected its own Starter and recorded why. On merge, the registry and Delivery
Notice must not continue to say the two governing documents **render** the definitions differently.

Preserve the finding as point-in-time evidence, but state that it was true on the Epic's execution
branch and was corrected by the Milestone Chat before merge. Remove or update all present-tense claims
in:

- `.ai-project/registry/fleet-registry.yml`'s citation note;
- Delivery Notice §3, §11 item 14, and Escalation Notice item 4 / its ask.

The CFO strings in the registry and tests are already correct and must not change.
