## Finding — required correction

**Location:** `P11-M36-E36.1__sweep-evidence.md` §4, and the mirroring sentence in
`P11-M36-E36.1__delivery-notice.md` §"The sweep, evidenced".

**Both state:**

> "Four bare `SN-23` strings do remain in `governance/`, **all inside the new allocation section**."

**Verified figure: seventeen.** Confirmed by two independent methods:

```
total SN-23 occurrences in governance/: 39
date-qualified:                         22
bare (total - dated):                   17
```

Thirteen of the seventeen are **outside** the allocation section — they are in the **four new
changelog rows this Epic itself authored**:

| Location | Bare occurrences | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| `AI-OPERATING-GUIDELINES.md:1042` (new v2.10.1 row) | 5 | identifier-naming; quoting the historical defective text |
| `artifact-communication-protocol.md:453` (new v1.4.1 row) | 3 | same |
| `fleet-operator.md:237` (new v1.2.1 row) | 3 | same |
| `fleet-operator-brief.md:292` (new v1.0.1 row) | 2 | same |
| `creation-chat-guide.md:145/151/154/166` | 4 | the four §4 already enumerates |

**The substance is not in question.** All thirteen are legitimately bare under the Epic's own stated
rule — they name the identifier (*"Two different Steering Notes hold `id: SN-23`"*), quote the
historical defective text (*"each cited «SN-23 Decision 2»"*), or describe the change class
(*"SN-23 citations date-qualified"*). **§4's reasoning is correct and extends to all seventeen
without modification.** The DoD item *"no un-dated SN-23 citation remains in the normative tier"* is
genuinely met — none of the seventeen is a citation.

**Why it still has to be corrected:**

1. **It defeats the property Judgment Call 3 paid for.** That call added the date form to two
   already-path-qualified citations, and its stated rationale was greppability — *"bare `SN-23` means
   unresolved, `SN-23 (date)` means resolved… exactly what lets a future sweep (or E36.5's audit)
   find stragglers without human judgment."* A future sweep applying that rule gets **17 hits and
   finds documented justification for 4.** The Epic argued the property was worth two changelog rows,
   then left thirteen undocumented exceptions to it.
2. **§4's heading is "deliberate, not missed."** Thirteen were missed by the enumeration — in the
   section whose entire purpose is to prove nothing was missed.
3. **E36.5's audit is a direct downstream consumer.** It reads this corpus for exactly this defect
   class. Handing it an evidence artifact that undercounts by thirteen sets it up to re-derive the
   same finding.

**Mitigating, and why this is rework rather than a re-run:** the **raw post-edit sweep output is
committed in full** at sweep-evidence §2, so nothing is hidden or falsified — the seventeen are
visible in the committed evidence. The defect is in the **analysis** of that output, not the output
itself. No re-sweep is needed.
