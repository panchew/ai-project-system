## What the Delivery Did Especially Well

Three things worth naming, because they are the reason this review is short.

**The metavariable discovery is a real contribution, not a workaround.** The first draft of the rules
instantiated the defects they prohibit — writing the prohibited forms out literally put 4 bare
identifiers and 1 milestone-key citation into `governance/`, and the Epic's own post-edit sweep caught
it. Solving it by describing the prohibited forms rather than exhibiting them makes the section
practise the rule it states. **The follow-on observation is more valuable still**: any future guard
asserting *"no bare identifier under `governance/`"* will trip on the rule document itself unless the
metavariable convention is enforced or that file is allowlisted. **That is a design constraint a guard
author could only otherwise learn by writing a broken guard**, and it is carried into M37's closure.

**The layer statement was made before the claim, not after.** `P11-GH-2` came into force two days ago
and this is its first delivery under it. The notice states which paths were swept for which claim, and
says plainly that the corpus outside `governance/` still holds bare identifiers in quantity and that
they are deliberately untouched. **A remediation claim scoped to its layer is exactly what the gap
record asks for.**

**The sweep was widened past the spec's three commands** — lowercase and spaced variants, level-keyed
families beyond escalation notices, bare milestone keys without a phase prefix — with every hit of the
loose pattern individually re-read and classified. The spec called its inventory a floor; the delivery
treated it as one.

## The Finding — a contradiction in the Epic spec, authored by me

**In-scope surfaces** authorized: *"Any escalation-notice-by-milestone-key citation the sweep finds
under `governance/`."*

**Do NOT touch** said: *"all ten of E37.1's other seeded documents."*

**`governance/systems/chat-hierarchy.md` is both.** The Epic applied *specific prohibition beats
general authorization*, left the citation, and escalated the decision to this chat rather than making
it silently. **On the text as written, that was the correct call**, and a spec that contradicts itself
should produce exactly this outcome rather than a unilateral choice in either direction.

**The contradiction is resolved in favour of the fix**, and recorded in the Epic spec at **v1.1.0**,
§Conflict resolution. The do-not-touch clause was written to protect **E37.1's work product** — front
matter, `version` fields, seeding rows — from a second epic landing in the same files. **It was never
meant to immunize an unrelated citation defect that happens to live in one of those ten documents**,
and reading it that way leaves M37's Acceptance Criterion 3 unmet in the directory's most-cited
document.

## The Epic's Second Reason — noted, not adopted

The delivery gave a second, independent reason to leave the citation: that amending the document would
oblige a version bump and changelog row *"in a document E37.1 owns in this milestone."*

**E37.1 is merged and closed; nothing is owned any more.** And the obligation runs the other way —
`chat-hierarchy.md` can now record its own amendment, which is precisely what E37.1 existed to make
possible. **Being able to record the change is an argument for making it, not against.** The reasoning
was careful and it is being overruled on a fact the Epic could not have been sure of at authoring
time, not on its quality.

## The Other Escalated Items — ruled, no rework

**1. The enforcement guard — confirmed: recommended, not built.** The Hard Constraint held under the
exact pressure the spec predicted, with the invariant now genuinely zero. **Carried to M37's Closure

so E37.1's versioning convention does not reach it at all** — as this Epic's own spec records under
Correction 2. **No bump was owed. Judgment confirmed.**

number* instruction has proved itself rather than merely being stated.
