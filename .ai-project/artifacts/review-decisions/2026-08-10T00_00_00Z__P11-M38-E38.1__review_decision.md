## Feedback — the single required rework

**Move Drivr's governance submodule from `governance/` to `.governance`.**

### Why, with the measurement

**`.governance` is the fleet convention, 8 to 3** — measured on the host, 2026-08-10, across every
enrolled project carrying a `.gitmodules`:

| Path | Count | Projects |
|---|---|---|
| **`.governance`** | **8** | `courtis`, `fieldledger-assesment`, `footboard`, `Getawayinsured2023`, `home_finance`, `local-agent-runner`, `panchew-io`, `voicebox` |
| `governance` | **3** | `ai-project-system-mcp`, `social-stories-creator`, `drivr` |

**The three exceptions are exactly the `init`-created projects.** The tool has been quietly producing
the minority form on everything it touches.

### This was not the Epic's choice, and the Epic recorded it correctly

`bin/ai-project-init` **hard-codes `governance` at three sites** — `:262` (the yml field), `:294`
(`git submodule add -f "$governance_source" governance`), `:298` (the `.gitmodules` branch hint) — and
**exposes no option for it**; its flags are only `--dir`, `--governance-source`,
`--governance-version`, `--skip-git`, `--skip-submodule`, `--help`, `--version`.

**E38.1 was instructed to enroll via `init` and instructed not to fix `init`. It complied, and it
flagged the deviation plainly in Drivr's README.** That was correct behaviour against the spec it was
given.

> **The defect is the Epic spec's and the tool's, not the Epic's.** v1.0.0 measured what `init`
> *defaults to* and framed it as *"not a defect to fix here — it is a fact to record."* **It never
> measured what the fleet uses.** Had it, the 8-to-3 majority would have been visible and the
> correction specified up front. **That instruction is withdrawn at spec v1.2.0.**

### Why correct it now rather than record it

**It is cheap now and a migration later.** At delivery Drivr has **2 commits** and **6 shallow textual
references** to the path — `.gitmodules`, `.ai-project.yml` (×3), `README.md` (×2), and
`pyproject.toml`'s `norecursedirs`. **Once E38.2 lands adapter code and E38.3's registry records the
path, a rename becomes a migration.**

**And the name collision plausibly contributed to FM 12.** `install_hq_agent` reads
`<target>/governance/agents/…` — written as though `governance/` meant *the framework's own governance
directory*, which is precisely its meaning **inside this repository**, rather than *the submodule root*.
Drivr's own config already carries the awkward result: `governance/governance/ai-project-yml-spec.md`.
**Under `.governance` the two levels are visually distinguishable rather than an identical repeated
word.**

## Required rework — five steps, all in Drivr

**This is Drivr work and therefore E38.1's.** The Milestone Chat does not write Drivr and has not
touched it.

1. **`git mv governance .governance`**, with `.gitmodules` updated — **the `path` *and* the
   `submodule."<name>"` section key** — so `git submodule status` resolves after the move.
2. **`.ai-project.yml`: `submodule_path: .governance/`**, and every in-file path comment updated,
   **including the `# Spec:` line at `:3`**.
3. **`pyproject.toml`: `norecursedirs`** updated to the new path.
4. **`README.md`: remove the deviation note** — it documents a deviation that no longer exists.
   Replace it with **one line** recording that the path is `.governance` per fleet convention and that
   `init` produces `governance/` and **was not modified**.
5. **Re-verify after the move**, and show it: §4 validity, `git submodule status` resolving, the
   installed agent's **sha256 unchanged**, and **Drivr's baseline still `2 passed`**.

> **⚠ Step 5's baseline check is not a formality.** The `norecursedirs` edit is **exactly** the change
> that silently starts collecting the vendored submodule's suite. **A baseline that grows is a failed
> rework, not a bonus.**
