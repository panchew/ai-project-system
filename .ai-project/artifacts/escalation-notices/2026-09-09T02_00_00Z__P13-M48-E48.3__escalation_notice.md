## The five remaining findings (as received, verbatim by substance)

1. **High — the ruling's blanket clean-worktree requirement was narrowed.** Ordinary `--remote`
   enrollment without a local-only record still mutates `origin`/upstream without requiring a clean
   worktree. (`spec:163-166,187-192`)
2. **High — credential-inspection precedence is ambiguous** for a stored credential-bearing `origin`
   plus a local-only record: the four-case table selects `verification-failure`, while the credential
   rule requires `invalid-url`. Credential inspection must be stated to precede **every** query
   branch. (`spec:112-119,139-148,204-208`)
3. **High — the visual does not carry the fixed mutation lifecycle.** `--remote` bypasses the
   URL/origin preflight and routes directly to verification; local-only-record removal, the
   transition commit, the resulting `HEAD`, the retained failure state, and retry are absent.
   (`.mmd:17-31`)
4. **Medium — regression and DoD obligations do not explicitly cover several ruled invariants:**
   read-only query exits, fetch-vs-push URL inspection, all precedence combinations, blanket
   dirty-worktree rejection, staged-only exact commits, transition `HEAD`, usage-exit precedence, and
   ambiguity combinations. (`spec:230-247,263-299`)
5. **Medium — the Starter still delegates settled design choices**, including `--remote` vs a
   post-init step and the disposition-artifact shape, despite the ruling fixing command modes,
   initializer placement, path, and schema. (`starter:260-263`)
