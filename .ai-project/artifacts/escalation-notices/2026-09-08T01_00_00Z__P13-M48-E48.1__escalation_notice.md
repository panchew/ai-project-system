## Why these are beyond this chat — state, not just wording

**Finding 1 is a repository-state/handoff defect, not a spec-line defect.** Measured locally on
2026-09-08: `milestone/M48` and `phase/P13` both descend from `d93a944`; the ruling commit `125bf58`
sits on `phase/P13` only and was **never merged or cherry-picked into `milestone/M48`**. Therefore
`git show milestone/M48:docs/phases/.../P13-M48__milestone-spec.md` still yields the v1.0.0 text
with **no `§Phase Ruling` section**. This chat's E48.1 spec (v1.4.0) cited that section against a
branch that does not carry it. Fixing it is not a re-edit of the Epic spec — it requires the amended
governing spec `125bf58` to reach `milestone/M48` (a parent-side or branch-synchronization act), and
re-doing that synchronization is this chat's to propose, not to silently perform under an exhausted
budget.

**Findings 2, 3, 4 are this chat's own rework defects**, acknowledged plainly:
- **Finding 2** — in the `+1` edit this chat over-rotated the Phase Ruling's "source-repository
  ownership" into assigning the *instantiated* E48.4/E48.5 evidence artifacts and manifest to
  `epic/E48.1`. The ruling keeps E48.1 at the *contract specification* level; the instantiated bars
  and manifests are E48.4/E48.5's, under `.ai-project/artifacts/agentic-runs/P13-M48-<E>/` in this
  repository on those Epics' own `epic/E48.4`/`epic/E48.5` branches — not E48.1's branch.
- **Finding 3** — the bar schema's `acceptance_criteria` field cites "path and branch" but dropped the
  **immutable target SHA** the ruling requires alongside them.
- **Finding 4** — the Starter's flag line conflated two distinct ruling clauses: *unknown tools*
  remain executed rounds (counted for C-A, not relabeled mutating), whereas *missing/malformed
  tool/result/`files_changed` evidence* yields `undetermined`/`ERROR`. Written together they read as
  a contradiction.

None of these is re-submitted here: the budget is exhausted and the Phase Chat's decision states no
further rework is authorized.
