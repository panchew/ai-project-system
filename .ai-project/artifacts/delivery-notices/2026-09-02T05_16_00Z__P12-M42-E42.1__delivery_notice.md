# Delivery Notice: P12-M42-E42.1 — Sandbox Absence Fails Closed

## Summary

**The one `FileNotFoundError` path in `bin/ai-project-orchestrator` that ignored the file's own
convention now applies it.** The `:396-401` Docker-absent fallback ran the model's command
unsandboxed on the host (`shell=True`) and said nothing in the record. It is **removed**, not made
louder: Docker-absent now raises a `SandboxUnavailable` sentinel that the caller routes through the
file's already-present G1 shape — state the reason, escalation report, archive the trigger file,
`sys.exit(LOCAL_UNAVAILABLE_EXIT)`. The epic's delegated decision is **removal of the host path
entirely — no opt-in survives**; a surviving opt-in, however well recorded, would be a door back to
the exact disposition this milestone exists to close, in the first script the agentic lane runs
through. "Docker is available" is defined as binary-present **and** daemon-reachable; a daemon
installed-but-not-running is its own fail-closed case with its own stated reason, and an ordinary
in-container failure is pinned by negative control to never be misread as a daemon outage.
